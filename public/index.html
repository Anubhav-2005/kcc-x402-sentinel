The security boundary

The request and the permission come from different places.

A merchant can describe a valid x402 payment. Only the buyer can authorize the amount, destination, resource, and time window. Sentinel enforces that separation at the wallet boundary.

01 / Observe

Read the 402

Capture the merchant’s actual offer, including amount, payee, asset, network, and resource.

02 / Bind

Check signed intent

Compare every offered term with the owner’s short-lived permit and wallet policy.

03 / Decide

Guard the signer

Reserve one-use permission and budget before a dry-run authorization is created.

What the referee measures

Show the safe purchase. Then change one term.

The referee compares Red’s submission with a server-held original. A blocked attack and a valid payment are both needed to judge the guard.
