02 /THE BOUNDARY

The merchant can ask. Only the buyer can authorize.

Sentinel sits at the point where a payment request would become a wallet authorization. It compares the actual demand with a separate, signed permission.

01

Read the 402

Take the amount, payee, asset, network, resource, and timeout from the merchant’s response.

02

Bind to intent

Check those fields against the buyer’s short-lived permit and wallet policy.

03

Guard the signer

Reserve the permit and budget before a dry-run authorization is created.
